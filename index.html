App preview

📡

IP logger with context

Every address change is captured with country, city, ISP, ASN org, timezone, and coordinates. History stays on your device: no cloud, no sync, no server.

🔎

DNS leak detection

Checks which DNS servers your device is actually using, natively in the app. It runs only when you start it. Clean pass or leak, no ambiguity.

🗂️

Filter, export, own your data

Slice history by IP version, network type, or country. Export filtered results to CSV. Import to restore. Your data, your format.

⚡
Lightweight. Background monitoring and real-time tracking are both off by default. When running, Tunnel'd holds 20–55 MB of memory depending on working mode. Tested on Samsung S25 (Android 16) and Samsung A32 (Android 13).

Built like a tool,
not like an ad platform.

Many "IP checker" apps are data vacuums with a pretty UI. They log your queries, bundle analytics SDKs, and monetise what should be a simple utility.

Tunnel'd stores everything locally in a SQLite database on your device. No analytics, no crash reporters, no telemetry. Every network request is user-triggered: opt-in monitoring, IP lookups, VirusTotal scans and DNS leak tests. The providers involved are documented in the privacy policy.

The source is GPLv3. Read every line, build it yourself, and check that it does what it claims.

Data flows, third-party IP providers and trust boundaries are covered in the Privacy Policy and Security & Trust Boundaries.

How it works

1

Install & open

Download the signed APK from GitHub Releases. It works out of the box; Android 13+ will ask once for notification permission.

2

It watches

With monitoring on (off by default), every change of public IP (new Wi‑Fi, VPN toggle, mobile data switch) is logged with full context.

3

You stay informed

Notification on every change. Searchable history with filters. DNS leak test on demand. Export whenever you want.
